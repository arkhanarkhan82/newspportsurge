The MMA schedule is packed with cards from the UFC, PFL, Bellator (now under PFL), ONE Championship, and regional promotions. Sportsurge aggregates upcoming MMA events with main-card times and Watch links so you can follow every fight night in one place.

Upcoming MMA Events

UFC Fight Nights run most weekends, with numbered pay-per-view cards several times a year. PFL seasons add playoff tournaments, and ONE Championship cards often start early for US time zones. The table above pulls live and upcoming bouts by promotion.

  • UFC Fight Night – weekly cards on ESPN platforms
  • UFC pay-per-view – numbered events with title fights
  • PFL – seasonal tournament format with playoffs
  • ONE Championship – MMA, Muay Thai, and kickboxing cards

See also the dedicated UFC schedule for fight cards focused on the Octagon, and the full schedule for every sport today.

How MMA Cards Are Structured

A typical card has a preliminary undercard and a main card. Main events are five rounds in championship or headliner bouts; most fights are three rounds. Broadcast partners in the US include ESPN, ESPN+, and PPV providers for numbered UFC events.

Popular Weight Classes

From heavyweight down to strawweight, fans follow champions across the men’s and women’s divisions. Featured names rotate as title fights are booked, so check each event page for the current lineup.

How to Watch MMA Events

Broadcast options depend on the promotion. UFC cards stream on ESPN+ in the United States, with numbered pay-per-view events sold through the same app. PFL and ONE Championship rights rotate by season; regional shows often use dedicated streaming platforms. Confirm each event’s listing in the table for the start time in your timezone and any available watch link.

Because main cards can run long with undercard finishes, note the estimated main-event time so you do not miss the headliner. Preliminary bouts frequently produce early stoppages that move the main card ahead of schedule.
