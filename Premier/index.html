Top Teams to Watch

Arsenal and Manchester City have set the pace at the top of the table in recent seasons, while Liverpool, Chelsea, and Manchester United remain in the European places. At the other end, promoted sides fight to stay up each May. Clubs like Aston Villa, Brighton, West Ham, and Brentford regularly challenge for Europe.

How to Watch Premier League in the USA

Premier League rights in the United States are held by NBC Sports and Peacock, with selected matches on USA Network and CNBC. Use the Sportsurge schedule to confirm kickoff times and available watch links for each matchweek.

Premier League Standings and Storylines

Each season the Premier League table takes shape over 38 rounds. The top four (plus cup winners) earn UEFA Champions League places; fifth and sixth can mean Europa League or Conference League. The bottom three are relegated to the Championship, which makes every spring weekend high-stakes for clubs in the drop zone.

International breaks pause the calendar two or three times a year, and the winter fixture pileup (Boxing Day and New Year’s matches) is a staple of the English season. Check back on this page before each matchweek for updated kickoff times and watch links.
