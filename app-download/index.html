Why Is the Sportsurge App Not on Google Play?

The Sportsurge app is currently distributed directly as an APK instead of through the Google Play Store.

When an application is installed from an APK outside Google Play, Android treats it as an installation from an external or unknown source. This is why Android may require you to enable an additional installation permission.

Android and Google Play Protect may also display additional warnings when applications are installed outside Google Play.

These warnings are security features designed to help users understand that an application is being installed from outside the normal Google Play distribution channel.
